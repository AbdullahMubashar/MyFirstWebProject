Little-Weaver: Hornet

Hornet is the half-spider, half-wyrm princess of Hallownest and the guardian of its ruins, famously introduced in the overgrown caverns of Greenpath. Born from a pact between the Pale King and Herrah the Beast, she is the half-sibling of the Knight but stands apart by possessing a mind, a voice, and a distinct identity. Armed with a needle and thread, she acts as a swift and agile gatekeeper who tests the Knight's strength to ensure they have the resolve needed to face the kingdom's fading curse.

Pure Vessel: The Hollow Knight

The Hollow Knight is a tragic, imposing figure chosen by the Pale King to serve as a living vessel tasked with sealing away the Radiance and saving the kingdom of Hallownest from a devastating plague. Raised in the depths of the Abyss and entirely devoid of voice or emotion, this tragic warrior was intended to be pure enough to contain the infection forever. However, due to a subtle emotional bond formed with their creator, a flaw marred their perfection, allowing the infection to gradually leak out and drive them mad. When encountered in the game, the Hollow Knight is a somber, infected shell of their former glory, chained within the Temple of the Black Egg, fighting a losing battle against the plague while aggressively striking out at anyone who approaches.
